What SSB Psych Test Does & How to Use It

Real SSB Psychology Mock Tests & Simulations with Strict Timers

SSB Psych Test gives you a real-time SSB psychology mock test and exam simulation with built-in timers. Prepare for PPDT, TAT, WAT, SRT, and Self Description (SD) Tests with realistic exam timers and an authentic SSB experience. Practice anytime from home, improve your writing speed and natural responses, and build the confidence you need to perform your best in the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview.

How to Get the Best Results (In-Depth Guide)

Most candidates fail the SSB psychology test not because they lack ideas, but because they run out of time or panic when the slide changes. At the selection board, a projector flashes pictures and words automatically. Nobody waits for you to finish writing. We built this platform to train your mind for that exact pressure.

1. Use a Pen and Blank Notebook

Do not type your answers on your phone or laptop. The real SSB test is 100% pen-and-paper. Keep an unruled notebook ready on your desk, place your screen in front of you like a projector, and write every story and sentence by hand.

2. Respect the Strict Timers

There is no pause button on our tests. If you miss a word during the 15-second WAT timer, leave that line blank in your notebook and immediately move to the next word. Never try to catch up on missed words, or you will ruin the rest of your test.

How Each Test Works on This Platform

  • PPDT (Picture Perception & Discussion Test): You get 30 seconds to look at a hazy picture, 1 minute to note the characters and action, and 4 minutes to write your story. After the timer stops, stand up and narrate your story aloud in under 1 minute. (Read PPDT Guide)
  • TAT (Thematic Apperception Test): You will see 11 pictures back-to-back followed by 1 blank slide. Each slide gives you 30 seconds to observe and 4 minutes to write a realistic, positive story. (Read TAT Guide)
  • WAT (Word Association Test): 60 words flash on your screen, one every 15 seconds. Write the first natural, meaningful sentence that comes to your mind. (Read WAT Guide)
  • SRT (Situation Reaction Test): You get 30 minutes to read and write practical solutions to 60 everyday situations. Keep your answers short, clear, and action-focused. (Read SRT Guide)
  • SD (Self Description Test): You get 15 minutes to write what your parents, teachers/employers, friends, and you yourself think about you, plus what qualities you want to improve. (Read SD Guide)
  • Full Mock (Complete Psych Test): Combines TAT, WAT, SRT, and SD in one continuous sitting just like Day 2 at the SSB board. Take this at least twice a week to build mental stamina.

Review Your Performance with PDF Downloads

Because our instant tests generate random pictures and words every time, you get an option to download the question paper PDF at the end of your session. Take a short break after your test, open your notebook alongside the PDF, and check whether your stories make sense, whether your reactions are practical, and where you hesitated.
